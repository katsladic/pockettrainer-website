Pocket Trainer Support

Need help with Pocket Trainer? We're here for you.

Frequently asked questions

I forgot my password. How do I reset it?

On the sign-in screen, enter your email address, then tap Forgot password?. We'll email you a link to set a new password. If it doesn't arrive within a few minutes, check your spam or junk folder.

How do I switch between kg and lb, or km and miles?

Go to Profile → Settings and choose your weight and distance units. You can also switch distance units for an individual cardio session while you log it.

I closed the app in the middle of a workout. Did I lose it?

No. Pocket Trainer saves your in-progress workout, so you can pick up where you left off the next time you open the app.

I'm not getting workout reminders.

Reminders are sent for workouts you schedule in the app. Make sure notifications are turned on for Pocket Trainer in your phone's settings:

  • iPhone: Settings → Notifications → Pocket Trainer
  • Android: Settings → Apps → Pocket Trainer → Notifications

Who can see my progress photos and body stats?

Only you. Your photos, measurements, workouts and custom exercises are private to your account. We never sell your data or use it for advertising. See our Privacy Policy for details.

The app can't use my camera or photos.

Pocket Trainer only asks for camera and photo access when you add a photo or video. If you declined earlier, you can turn it on in your phone's settings under Pocket Trainer.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account and all your data, including workouts, history, progress records, photos and videos. You can also email us at pockettrainerhq@gmail.com to ask us to delete it.

Is Pocket Trainer free?

Yes, Pocket Trainer is free to download and use.

Health disclaimer

Pocket Trainer is designed for general fitness and is not a substitute for professional medical advice. Talk to your doctor before starting a new exercise program, and stop exercising if you feel pain, dizziness or shortness of breath.
