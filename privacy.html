Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

About this policy

This policy explains what personal information the Pocket Trainer mobile app collects, why, and how you can control it.

  • Applies to: the Pocket Trainer iOS and Android apps and the accounts used to sign in to them.
  • Contact: pockettrainerhq@gmail.com

Information we collect

We collect only what you enter or upload in the app. The app does not use analytics, advertising or crash-reporting tools. Our website uses Google Analytics to understand visits; the app does not.

Category What it includes Required?

Stored only on your device: your sign-in session (so you stay logged in), a small cache of your personal bests, and workout reminder notifications, which are scheduled locally by your phone.

Device permissions: the app asks for camera and photo library access only when you choose to take or attach a photo or video. You can change these in your device settings at any time.

How we use your information

We use your information only to run the app for you:

  • Create and secure your account, and keep you signed in.
  • Show, save and sync your workouts, schedule, workout history, progress and goals.
  • Calculate things like personal records, last weight used and workout totals.
  • Display weights and distances in the units you chose.
  • Send account emails you ask for, such as password reset links.
  • Remind you of scheduled workouts (reminders are created on your device).
  • Respond to your support requests.

We rely on your consent and on the need to provide the service you signed up for. We do not make automated decisions about you that have legal or similar effects.

Where your data is stored and who processes it

Your data is stored in our own database (PocketBase) hosted on Google Cloud in the United States (US Central region, Iowa). These service providers process it only on our behalf:

Provider Purpose Data involved
Google Cloud Hosts our servers and database All account, fitness, photo and video data
Cloudflare Secures and routes traffic between the app and our servers Data in transit, IP address
Resend Sends password reset and account emails Email address
Apple / Google Distribute the app through their app stores Governed by their own privacy policies
YouTube (Google) Plays exercise videos that link to YouTube When you play one, YouTube may collect information under Google's Privacy Policy

Workout reminders are scheduled on your phone, not through a push notification service. We do not share your information with anyone else unless required by law.

We never sell your data or use it for advertising

  • We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information.
  • We do not use your fitness, health or body information for advertising, marketing or profiling.
  • We do not share your information with data brokers or advertisers.
  • Your custom exercises, photos and videos are private to your account.

How long we keep your data, and how to delete it

We keep your information for as long as your account exists. You can delete individual items (workouts, activities, custom exercises, photos) at any time in the app.

Delete your account: go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account. This immediately and permanently deletes your account and all associated data, including workouts, workout history, schedule, progress records, photos, videos and custom exercises. It also removes workout reminders from your device.

Copies may remain in encrypted server backups, which are kept only for disaster recovery and are automatically overwritten within 7 days. We do not restore or use deleted data from backups for any other purpose. You can also email pockettrainerhq@gmail.com to request deletion.

Your privacy rights

You can, at any time:

  • Access and correct your information directly in the app, or ask us for a copy of it.
  • Delete your account and data in the app, or by emailing us.
  • Withdraw consent by deleting optional information or your account.
  • Complain to a privacy regulator.

Depending on where you live, privacy laws give you these rights. In Canada this is PIPEDA (Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada). If you are in the EU/UK, GDPR also gives you rights to data portability, restriction and objection. If you are in California, the CCPA gives you the right to know and delete; we do not sell or share personal information as defined by the CCPA.

To make a request, email pockettrainerhq@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days and may need to verify your identity.

Children

Pocket Trainer is not intended for children under 13 (16 in the EU/UK). We do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, email pockettrainerhq@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Security

We protect your information with encryption in transit (HTTPS), hashed passwords, and access rules that let each user see and change only their own data. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your information, we will notify you and the regulator as the law requires.

International transfers

Our providers may process data outside your province or country, including in the United States. When that happens, your data is protected by contracts and the safeguards described in this policy.

Changes to this policy

We will post any changes here with a new effective date. For significant changes, we will notify you in the app or by email before they take effect.
